Palette for Metal Crab.

Background colors are the same as pal/00, except the flash palettes.

Currently using two palettes:
5. Body, legs, claws.
7. Face.

Hopefully with sprite-level (instead of metasprite-level) attributes more colors can be added.

The boss damage flash effect is a washed-out version of the colors.

The projectile palette is a backwards blue set (white, light-blue, dark-blue), used for the boss defeated explosions to make them look like splashes of water.

The script flash effect is changed to a simple blue from the boss' main skin tone applied to the background. This is used at the beginning and end of the fight.

Shares metasprites with Ifrit.
